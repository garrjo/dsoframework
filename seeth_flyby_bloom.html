The Fork: Anisotropy Under Rotation

Standard particle mixing: κ²_T and κ²_L are fixed by Lorentz structure. Rotating the medium relative to the gradient source does not change the effective coupling.

Bloom: the gradient has orientation. The D-plane has a preferred direction set by the dominant mass. Rotating the measurement axis changes the effective coupling.
